Under the hood

What makes it feel real

Most stock-market games move a number randomly. This one models the parts that actually decide whether a trade was any good.

01 / Microstructure
📖

You trade against a book

Every company quotes a bid and an ask with depth behind it. You buy at the offer, sell at the bid, and a large order walks the ladder and fills at a worse average price. Size costs money, exactly as it should.

02 / Price process
📈

Prices have structure

A market factor drags the whole exchange around, sectors move together, and each name adds its own noise on top. Volatility clusters, prices revert toward fair value, and rare jumps arrive as news.

03 / Sessions
🔔

The market closes

Pre-market, regular hours, after-hours, weekends and holidays — on the real NYSE calendar. Trade thin sessions at your peril, and watch what the overnight gap does to the stop you left resting.
